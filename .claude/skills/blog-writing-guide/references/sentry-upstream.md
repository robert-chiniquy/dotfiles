# Sentry Blog Writing Skill

**The bar:** Every Sentry blog post should be something a senior engineer would share in their team's Slack, or reference in a technical decision.

## Banned Language

- "We're excited/thrilled to announce"
- "Best-in-class" / "industry-leading" / "cutting-edge"
- "Seamless" / "seamlessly"
- "Empower" / "leverage" / "unlock"
- "Robust" as decoration
- "At [Company], we believe..."
- "Streamline"
- Filler transitions: "That being said," "It's worth noting that," "At the end of the day," "Without further ado," "As you might know"
- "In this blog post, we will explore..."

## The Opening

The opening must do one of two things: **state the problem** or **state the conclusion**. Never start with background, company history, or hype.

## Structure: Follow the Reader's Questions

1. What problem does this solve?
2. How does it actually work?
3. What were the trade-offs or alternatives?
4. How do I use/try/implement this?

## Technical Quality

- Numbers over adjectives
- Code must work
- Diagrams for systems with more than two interacting components, labeled with real names
- Honesty over hype

## Section Headings Must Convey Information

Weak: Background, Architecture, Results, Conclusion.
Strong: a claim the section actually makes.

## AI Writing Patterns to Avoid

Staccato dramatic fragments. Bumper-sticker aphorisms. Three-beat reveals. Smug simplicity. Parallel structure ad copy. Personality only in the bookends.

## When Reviewing

Technical: claims accurate, code works, architecture matches reality.
Editorial: opening hooks, no corporate filler, headings convey information, title specific.
